 Lap charts

        
Each channel is shown on a separate chart. The dashed vertical lines mark where each lap starts and ends in the loaded data.

        

          

            
Speed: shows how fast the car is moving. High peaks are straights, and deep drops are braking zones before corners.

            
          

          

            
Throttle: shows accelerator pedal usage in percent. Near 100% means full power, near 0% means you are lifting off.

            
          

          

            
Brake: shows brake pedal pressure/usage. Spikes mean braking events and help identify where you brake too early or too late.

            
          

          

            
Gear: shows the selected gear over the lap. It helps you see shift timing and whether gear choice is consistent corner by corner.

            
          

        

      


      

        
 Lap comparison

        
Compare the same lap id (default lap-1) between two uploaded sessions for a quick summary. Use Load segment deltas on map after choosing reference and compare sessions to colour the schematic markers and show per-segment deltas. The segment model uses average speeds in each slice; it is a coaching-style hint, not a professional timing loop.
