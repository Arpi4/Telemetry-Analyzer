 Lap charts

        
Each channel is shown on a separate chart. To keep the view clean, only one lap is displayed at a time.

        

          
          
          
          Lap time: -
        

        

          

            
Speed: shows how fast the car is moving. High peaks are straights, and deep drops are braking zones before corners.

            
          

          

            
Throttle: shows accelerator pedal usage in percent. Near 100% means full power, near 0% means you are lifting off.

            
          

          

            
Brake: shows brake pedal pressure/usage. Spikes mean braking events and help identify where you brake too early or too late.

            
          

          

            
Gear: shows the selected gear over the lap. It helps you see shift timing and whether gear choice is consistent corner by corner.
