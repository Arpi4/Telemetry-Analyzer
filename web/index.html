 Lap charts

        
Each channel is shown on a separate chart, next to a map of the driven line. To keep the view clean, only one lap is displayed at a time.

        

          
          
          
          Lap time: -
        

        

          

            

              
Speed: shows how fast the car is moving. High peaks are straights, and deep drops are braking zones before corners.

              
            

            

              
Throttle: shows accelerator pedal usage in percent. Near 100% means full power, near 0% means you are lifting off.

              
            

            

              
Brake: shows brake pedal pressure/usage. Spikes mean braking events and help identify where you brake too early or too late.

              
            

            

              
Gear: shows the selected gear over the lap. It helps you see shift timing and whether gear choice is consistent corner by corner.

              
            

          

          

            
Track map: the driven line coloured by speed, from blue (slow) to red (fast). B1, B2, … mark where each braking zone starts, the white dot is start/finish. Hover a chart to follow the car.

            

          

        

      


      

        
 Lap comparison

        
Compare selected laps between two uploaded sessions. The summary shows lap-level difference, and the chart overlays both speed traces with different colors.

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        

          
        

        

          

            
Speed overlay: same chart, two laps. Red = reference, cyan = compare.

            
          

          

            
Time delta map: the reference lap's line, green where the compare lap gains time and red where it loses. S2, S3, … mark the sector starts, coloured by that sector's delta.
